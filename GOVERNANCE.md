# Governance

The `DDNA-Engineering` GitHub organization owns the public repository and its publication/release decisions. Repository maintainers review changes, manage releases, and control the private vulnerability-reporting channel. The broader product PRD and architecture remain proposed engineering baselines until an explicit versioned acceptance record says otherwise.

Public releases must contain only rights-cleared code, documentation, and synthetic or separately approved content. A merged change does not authorize publication of a standards document, establish project applicability, approve an engineering interpretation, or certify a deployment.

A release candidate must identify its task and requirement IDs, pass contract and acceptance checks, record fixture digests and limitations, document migrations and rollback, and receive maintainer approval. Security exceptions and content-rights decisions require an authorized human; the software and its agents cannot approve their own exceptions.
