EXAMPLE-SPEC-100 Revision B
Fictional Vehicle Adapter Environmental Specification

1 Scope
This synthetic specification defines retention evidence for a fictional vehicle adapter.

4.2.1 Connector retention
The adapter shall retain the connector under a steady axial load of 100 N for 60 seconds.

NOTE 1 — Governing condition
Apply the load after conditioning the assembly at -20 °C ± 2 °C for at least four hours.

4.2.2 Interface sealing
The adapter shall prevent visible water ingress during a 10-minute synthetic spray exposure.
