The acquisition manifest records each selected DLA document identifier, title, detail page, and current-component source URL, distribution marking, local path, byte size, and SHA-256. The source baseline records the selection rule and excluded or incomplete scope. The corpus index binds the compiled archives to the acquisition snapshot; the outer bundle manifest inventories the delivered files. These records allow a recipient to identify and verify the exact source set rather than relying on the software license or a general catalog label.

The included corpus contains only downloaded components classified by the recorded publisher metadata as Distribution Statement A. [DLA describes that marking](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qshelp.aspx) as approved for public release with unlimited distribution. The marking is a recorded publisher fact, not a grant of every processing, model-use, or republication right in every context. Restricted-only records are excluded. Records with both public and unavailable restricted components remain explicitly marked as partial; the bundle does not present those public components as a complete edition.

## Bundled tokenizer data

From 0.1.0a8, `tokenizer/o200k_base.json` holds the `o200k_base` byte-pair vocabulary that OpenAI publishes for its MIT-licensed `tiktoken` library, exported once with `tiktoken==0.14.0` and pinned by `provenance/tokenizer.json`. It is used only to count tokens for `select_evidence` budgets on this machine; it is not standards content, sends nothing anywhere, and grants no rights over the standards above. Its identity is checked at every model-connection start.
